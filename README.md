#  Problem Statement No. 5 — AI Against Misinformation

##  Background

An estimated **90% of online content could be synthetically generated by 2026**. Deepfake videos, AI-generated fake news, and cloned voice scams are rapidly eroding public trust. India alone saw over **500 million fake news shares** during recent election cycles.

Existing fact-checking systems are largely manual, slow, and unable to scale — especially across India's **22+ languages**, where automated detection remains limited or non-existent.

---

## ❗ Problem Statement

Build an **AI-powered system** capable of detecting, flagging, and countering misinformation in real time across multiple modalities:

- 📝 Text  
- 🖼️ Images  
- 🎙️ Audio  
- 🎥 Video  

The system should support at least **two Indian languages** (in addition to English) and may include:

- Deepfake Detection  
- AI-generated Content Classification  
- Automated Fact Verification Pipelines  
- Credibility Scoring Mechanisms  

---

##  Twist ( Additional Features )

Detection alone is not enough.

The system should also explore how misinformation can be:

- Contextualized  
- Explained  
- Countered in real time  

For example:

- Generating trustworthy summaries  
- Highlighting inconsistencies  
- Providing verifiable alternative information  

The envisioned outcome is an **AI-driven misinformation response workflow** that not only identifies suspicious content but also assists users, platforms, or authorities in making informed decisions — shifting from passive detection to active trust-building and misinformation mitigation at scale.

---

#  Features

-  **Multimodal Analysis**  
  Analyze text claims, URLs, images, and deepfake media.

-  **Multilingual Support**  
  Supports English, Hindi, and Tamil.

-  **AI-Powered Fact-Checking**  
  Uses advanced forensic signals to verify forwarded messages and media authenticity.

-  **Beautiful Dashboard**  
  Modern UI for viewing truth reports, credibility scores, and forensic analysis.
